# Phase 12C — HVAC smart-form comparison (baseline)

**Status: qualitative baseline only. No timed trial or monetizable savings demonstrated.**

## Same fictional case as the live Echo test

**Initial message:** "Our air conditioner runs, but the house isn't cooling. I'd like someone to check it."

**Real Echo follow-up before Phase 12C:** "How long has the air conditioner been running without cooling the house effectively?"

**Customer clarification:** "all day and blowing hot air"

**Real owner record:** classified HVAC, review promptly; highlighted thermostat setting as unknown; suggested "Review system configuration and operation" as next step. This was a real completed AI result with a generic owner instruction, not an example produced by the new prompts.

## What a good non-AI contact form could collect

A realistic ordinary service form could include these fields at no AI inference cost:

| Field | Sample response |
| --- | --- |
| Service requested | Heating and cooling / AC |
| Describe issue | AC runs, house is not cooling |
| What comes from vents? | Warm/hot air |
| When did it start? | All day |
| Thermostat mode and set temperature (optional) | Unknown / not supplied |
| Desired appointment time (optional) | Not supplied |
| Urgency description | Owner to assess |

This hypothetical form would produce almost the same actionable data as Echo's prior two-turn test. It is a design comparison, not an actual form built or timed against Echo.

## Specific areas AI must outperform

1. Understand a **genuinely ambiguous** issue without forcing a business-type dropdown.
2. Ask one useful question the static form did not ask; avoid repeating known information.
3. Leave the owner with a more specific, fact-grounded next step and unknown detail.
4. Avoid hallucinated diagnoses, promised appointments, and contacting a customer without any authorized contact method.
5. Reduce measured owner-review minutes or missed inquiries **after** accounting for AI failures, corrections, usage and operating costs.

**Finding from current evidence:** For the tested AC case, a simple well-designed form could have collected the main symptoms directly; Echo's informational advantage is **not established**. The new Phase 12C prompts are an untested attempt to improve quality, not evidence of improvement.

## Owner timing experiment (future, fictional)

For the next completed AI scenario, make a comparison using exactly the same customer facts. Have the owner review a simple form version and the AI version in counterbalanced order, time the decisions, and record how many clarifications are still needed. Repeat multiple trials. The earlier one-off 75/100 subjective estimate is not validated and cannot be treated as a baseline accuracy or product score.

**Commercial gate:** no premium service pricing claim until improvement in actionable outcomes and cost effectiveness is measured.
